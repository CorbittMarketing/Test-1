Our Story

26 Years of Smashing Goodness

It all started in 1998 when Kaelan fired up a single griddle on Whyte Ave. One simple belief: fresh, never frozen beef smashed thin and seared to perfection.

No fancy toppings. No frozen patties. Just real Alberta beef, smashed on a hot flattop, topped with melted cheese and served on a toasted bun.

Three generations later, we're still smashing burgers the same way. Because when something's this good, you don't mess with it.
